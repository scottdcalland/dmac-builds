Who we are

A local builder who turns up and does it right

DMac Builds is run by Danny Mac, a bricklayer based in Platt Bridge, Wigan. What started on the tools has grown into a firm that takes on bricklaying, landscaping, groundworks and extensions across the Wigan area.

  • A proper trade background. Bricklayers first, so the brick and block work is clean, level and built to last.
  • One team, start to finish. Footings to finished shell, without the gaps between trades.
  • Local and accountable. We work on our own doorstep, so our reputation travels with us.
